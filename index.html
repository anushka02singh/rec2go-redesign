Rec2Go, redesigned around what students come to do

A human-centered redesign of the Rutgers Recreation app, from an AI wireframing critique to student research, personas and a clickable Figma prototype.

The problem

Students open Rec2Go for a few quick, time-sensitive tasks: getting in the door, checking what's open, joining a class. The app is organized around the rec department's structure instead, so those answers take several taps, a trip to the website, or a guess.

9 of 19

home-screen tiles leave the app for a web page in a different visual style.

4

separate campus schedule screens, with no "open now" status or way to filter by activity.

1 tap

is all the member pass should take. Today it sits behind an unexplained "NetID / Local" choice, and the boldest button on that screen is "Clear Barcode."

Part 1: what AI got right and wrong

Before designing anything myself, I gave Figma Make and Google Stitch the same prompts in three rounds and critiqued what they produced.

Assumptions

Same generic template

With a vague prompt, both tools named the user "Jordan," invented a school and assumed a single building.

Facts

Confident, wrong details

Figma said Livingston closes at midnight. The real schedule that day said 9 PM.

Scope

Features nobody asked for

Equipment checkout, a staff override code, and 2-factor login added without being prompted.

Research

Live interviews weren't possible, so the research combines an anonymous student survey with real secondary evidence: my own use of the app, one student's written answers, a walkthrough of every Rec2Go tile, Rutgers Rec's published hours and guidance, and student reporting in The Daily Targum.

Use a Rutgers rec center? The survey takes about 5 minutes. It's anonymous, and nothing is sent anywhere: you copy your answers at the end and send them to me.

Prototype v1

Ten linked screens built in Figma. The layout borrows a familiar fitness-app pattern (profile top-left, pass top-right) and swaps the promo card for "Open now near you." Hours, spaces and classes are Rutgers Rec's real schedule for Sunday, Nov 8, 2026.

Core flows

  1. Get in: any screen → Pass. 1 tap.
  2. Find a court: Open now → Basketball → 4 courts with closing times.
  3. Book a class: Home → class → Book my spot → confirmed.

Build it yourself

The figma-plugin folder generates every screen as editable Figma layers with working prototype links.
